What a Company Brain is

A permissioned, versioned body of company knowledge that AI agents and people read before doing the work. Every item has a source, an owner and a verification date.

What goes inside

Three kinds of knowledge, kept apart so each stays easy to update.

Facts

What is true now: target architecture, coding standards, business rules, deprecation lists.

Decisions

Why you chose this way, with a date. Never rewritten, only superseded.

Playbooks

How work is done: releases, incidents, reviews, onboarding.

We extract it from what you already have: code, Jira, Confluence, ADRs and runbooks. Nothing is written back to your systems by default.

How it works

  1. Connect. Read-only connectors pull from your existing tools.
  2. Structure. Knowledge is sorted into facts, decisions and playbooks, and loaded by the type of work.
  3. Check. Every AI output is tested against your written rules. Violations are blocked, the rule is cited, and an audit trail is written.
  4. Improve. A weekly report shows what was broken or missing. Humans decide what changes.

How it fits into AI-DLC

The Company Brain is the first layer of AI-DLC, Kloia’s framework for AI across the delivery lifecycle. The brain holds the knowledge. AI-DLC puts agents on top of it at every phase, from Define to Operate, with people making the decisions that matter.

You can start with the brain alone, or alongside a modernisation, new build or QA workflow.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from a wiki or RAG?

A wiki is read when someone remembers it, and RAG retrieves text that matches a question. A Company Brain is loaded into the work by default, keeps the reasoning behind decisions, and has its output checked against rules.

Do we have to write everything down first?

No. We extract from existing code, tickets, ADRs and documents, and people confirm what matters.

Does it replace Jira, Confluence or GitHub?

No. It connects read-only and complements them.

Who stays in control?

People. Agents suggest and audit, and critical decisions always go to human review.

Can we use our own or an on-premise model?

Yes. It is model-agnostic, including AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI and on-premise models.

Is it suitable for regulated sectors?

Yes. Audit trails are automatic and human approval is kept throughout.

Is it only for large teams?

It creates the most value for engineering teams of fifty or more. A focused starter package exists for smaller teams.
