What a Company Brain is

A Company Brain is an AI that has learned how your company works, and works inside those rules. It is not a chatbot on top of your files, and it is not a search bar.

It learns three things:

  1. Your rules. Governance rules, internal rules and every policy, in one place.
  2. Your data. Pulled from every system that holds it, without moving it or changing it.
  3. Your history. Past projects, customers, decisions and results.

Then it calculates, answers and drafts the way your company would, and shows which rule and which source it used.

How it works

  1. Learn. We collect your policies, governance rules and internal rules, and turn them into rules the brain can apply.
  2. Connect. Read-only connections to the systems that hold your data. Nothing is changed in them.
  3. Work. The brain answers, calculates and drafts, and cites the rule and the source behind each result.
  4. Approve. A person reviews anything that matters. The brain proposes, you decide.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers from documents. A Company Brain applies your rules, calculates from your live data, drafts in your standards, and shows how it got there.

Do we need an IT team to use it?

No. Your team asks in plain language. Kloia handles the connections and setup.

Does it replace our ERP, CRM or other systems?

No. It connects to them read-only and leaves them as they are.

Can it be wrong?

Any AI can. That is why each result cites its rule and source, and why a person approves anything important.

Who can see what?

The brain follows your access rules, so people only see what they are already allowed to see.

Which brain should we start with?

Usually the function with the most manual number-pulling or rule-checking. We help you choose in the first conversation.

Can we use our own AI model?

Yes. It works with private and on-premise models, so data can stay in your environment.
